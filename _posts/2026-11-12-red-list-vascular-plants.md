Research sections: Plant population biology, Botany — project leads: Guy Colling, Laura Daco (research strategy "Understanding and preserving nature").

Rapid environmental and climate change can cause plant species to decline or even go extinct. Red Lists are important tools for monitoring biodiversity and for developing species conservation strategies.

The updated Red List of the vascular plants present in Luxembourg helps to better understand the impact of environmental and climate change since the first publication of the national Red List in 2005, and plays an important role in addressing the current challenges of biodiversity loss.

## Key results

The new Red List is based on 1,423 native and naturalised taxa. No threat category was assigned to the 172 naturalised exotic taxa (EA) or to the 79 taxa for which data were deficient (DD). Of the remaining 1,172 assessed taxa:

- 9.0 % are considered regionally extinct (RE)
- 8.3 % critically endangered (CR)
- 14.0 % endangered (EN)
- 13.0 % vulnerable (VU)
- 9.8 % near threatened (NT)

Compared with the 2005 Red List, the share of threatened taxa (CR + EN + VU) in Luxembourg increased from 30.4 % to 35.2 %.
