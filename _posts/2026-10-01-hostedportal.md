We are pleased to announce the launch of **biodiversiteit.lu**, Luxembourg's new GBIF-hosted portal for biodiversity data.

## Discover Luxembourg's biodiversity

The portal provides a central access point for discovering and exploring biodiversity data related to Luxembourg. By making information about species occurrences and their distribution more accessible, biodiversiteit.lu aims to support:

- Scientific research and biodiversity monitoring
- Nature conservation and environmental management
- Education and public awareness
- Evidence-based decision-making

## Connecting biodiversity data

Hosted by the [Global Biodiversity Information Facility (GBIF)](https://www.gbif.org/), the portal connects users with biodiversity data published by participating institutions and data providers.

It offers a starting point for **researchers, naturalists, students, policymakers**, and anyone interested in exploring Luxembourg's rich natural heritage.

## What's next?

This launch marks an important first step towards improving the visibility and accessibility of biodiversity information in Luxembourg.

As the portal develops, we hope to:

- Encourage more organisations to publish their biodiversity data.
- Increase the visibility of existing datasets.
- Promote collaboration between biodiversity data providers and users.
- Share updates about biodiversity data and related initiatives in Luxembourg.

> Every dataset contributes to a better understanding of biodiversity and helps build a more complete picture of our natural environment.

## Explore the portal

Visit [**biodiversiteit.lu**](https://biodiversiteit.lu) to explore the portal and discover the biodiversity data available.

**Stay tuned** for further updates, new datasets, and stories about biodiversity data and the people working to document and protect nature in Luxembourg.
