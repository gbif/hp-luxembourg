Why are Red Lists so important for biodiversity conservation? In this interview, our Head of Conservation Biology, Axel Hochkirch, explains how Red Lists help us understand the threats facing species and why they are essential tools for guiding conservation efforts.

The interview was conducted by Florian Pirvulet, one of our Science Club members — and the video was produced by António Cruz. We are also delighted to see our Science Club members taking an active interest in scientific topics and engaging directly with researchers!
