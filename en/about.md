## Our mission

Conservation Hub Luxembourg brings together, on a single platform, all information on biodiversity and natural heritage conservation in Luxembourg. The hub aggregates previously isolated sources of information and makes new documents — until now barely accessible to the public — openly available.

Biodiversity loss is not only a loss of natural heritage, but also a profound loss of cultural heritage — and a threat to the economy. Yet the general public is not always aware of the essential role the **Naturmusée** plays in biodiversity conservation, and conservation-related activities and information have not always been structured and synergetic.

The hub addresses this: a user-friendly platform, developed alongside a new team at the museum, that structures conservation resources, makes them accessible to everyone, and acts as a catalyst for interoperability at the national level. It is also intended to be at the centre of a biennial national conference serving as a networking event for all national actors.
