*(Scientific name: *Quercus robur*)*

### **Habitat and Distribution**
*Quercus robur* is native to Europe and thrives in deciduous forests, parks, and river valleys.

---

### **Seasonal Cycle**
- **Spring**: New leaves emerge; tree flowers.
- **Summer**: Crown fully developed; acorns form.
- **Autumn**: Acorns mature; leaves shed.
- **Winter**: Leafless; bark and buds visible.

---

### **Ecological Importance**
Old oaks support diverse ecosystems, providing food and habitat for insects, birds, and mammals.

---

### **Identification Summary**
Look for:
- Broad, spreading crown.
- Deeply lobed leaves with short stalks.
- Acorns on long stalks.
