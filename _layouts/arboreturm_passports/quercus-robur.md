# English Oak

## *Quercus robur*

The English oak is a large deciduous tree native to much of Europe.

### Identification

- **Leaves:** Lobed, with a very short stalk.
- **Flowers:** Small greenish flowers appearing in spring.
- **Fruit:** Acorns.

### Distribution

Native to much of Europe.

### Habitat

Found in woodland, hedgerows and open landscapes.

### Phenology

- **Flowering:** April–May
- **Fruiting:** September–October

### Did you know?

The English oak can live for several hundred years.
